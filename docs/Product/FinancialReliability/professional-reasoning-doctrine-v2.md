# Professionally bounded usefulness — reviewed doctrine v2

Authority: Founder-transmitted independent professional review, preserved in
`review-source-v2.txt`, sections 1–5 and 18, with FR09/10/11/12 specific consequences.
Status: ADOPTED_PROFESSIONAL_REQUIREMENTS / IMPLEMENTATION_PARTIAL / NOT_GATE_PASS.
This is a governed requirement, not proof of a new reasoning engine or ontology.

## PR-01 — Reasoning integrity, not just number integrity

Correct calculations can support professionally wrong conclusions. Prevent
premature causality, correlation-as-explanation, incomplete diagnosis-as-certainty,
arithmetic-as-trend, cause-as-desirability, diagnosis-as-action, unjustified ratio
labels, missing-as-zero, silent reconciliation, assumed comparability, irrelevant
accessible context and conclusions beyond evidence scope.

## PR-02 — No automatic epistemic promotion

OBSERVATION -> SIGNAL -> HYPOTHESIS -> INVESTIGATION -> ESTABLISHED CAUSALITY
-> ECONOMIC IMPACT -> OPTIONS -> DECISION.

Each promotion needs appropriate evidence; stop honestly when it is absent.
Prudence is not silence: professionally relevant unproven relationships may be
explicit signals/hypotheses to investigate, never silently established causes.
Numerical coincidence plus a plausible mechanism does not establish causality.
Established causality does not establish profitability or justify an action.
Human professional responsibility remains reserved (Constitution Article II;
Profession Model chapters 7–8). This chain does not automatically create domain
objects or retroactively invalidate human conditional decisions. It governs
automatic reasoning promotion, not a new prohibition on explicit human judgment
under uncertainty. No implicit DecisionArc, ExpectedImpact, outcome or learning.

## PR-03 — Useful UNKNOWN

UNKNOWN -> MISSING EVIDENCE -> PRIORITIZED INFORMATION REQUEST -> NEW EVIDENCE
-> RE-EVALUATION. When useful, identify the minimum relevant missing evidence.
Requests must be decision-oriented, proportionate, prioritized and operationally
minimal. Do not request an entire ERP/accounting/CRM universe by default. A
request is not obtained evidence, and obtained evidence is not automatic resolution.

## PR-04 — Distinct epistemic/access states

- AMBIGUOUS: meaning cannot sufficiently be established.
- UNKNOWN: the requested conclusion cannot be established from available evidence.
- CONTRADICTION: understood claims are mutually incompatible; not low confidence.
- FORBIDDEN: caller/context lacks authorization to access evidence.
- EMPTY: authorized legitimate scope has no relevant evidence.

Never collapse these states or equate absence of evidence with evidence of absence.
Carry contradiction to dependent conclusions; do not necessarily discard independent
facts. Temporal selection must not become hidden conflict resolution. Security may
use an opaque not-found response to avoid disclosure; it must not internally or
at terminal consumption turn a refused read into a successful empty dossier.

## PR-05 — Calculation is not professional semantics

A valid calculation does not license its professional label: unequal-period
difference is not growth; spend/revenue co-movement ratio is not ROI/ROAS/return;
mechanical annualization is not a forecast; receivables are not cash. Asset,
liquidity and immediately available liquidity differ. Historical comparability
does not establish predictive capability. A deterministic layer may calculate
while the professional layer refuses the unsupported interpretation. Keep source
reported and deterministic derived values, provenance, discrepancy and unresolved
CONTRADICTION together; neither overwrites/suppresses the other.

## PR-06 — Governed comparability

Duration, period, perimeter, entity scope, accounting convention, source/metric
definition and restatement status can each constrain comparison. Arithmetic may
be available while economic comparability is UNKNOWN or NON-COMPARABLE. Reconciling
one dimension never resolves another automatically. All relevant reconciliations
need provenance. No manufactured comparable residual or inferred annual boundaries.

## PR-07 — Evidence-bounded scope before intelligence

Every conclusion inherits its evidence scope: tenant, client/entity, engagement,
analysis, period, source and decision/memory context as relevant. Authorization
and reasoning relevance are separate. Accessible entity B must not contaminate
selected entity A. Cross-tenant evidence is excluded BEFORE context construction;
an LLM instructed to ignore foreign evidence is not an isolation control.
Apply to facts, sources, derivations, hypotheses, contradictions, temporal history,
decisions, actions, memory, citations, UI and exports. No silent scope promotion.

## PR-08 — Evidence-calibrated acceptance

Reviewed expectations != executed output != approved output != gate PASS.
No aggregate score hides a critical failure. No scenario-specific production
branch, example answer or prompt may teach the implementation to pass this oracle.
No ontology expansion merely to pass FR10. Refusal covers only the capability
actually absent; it is not a substitute for broader required usefulness. No real
data, provider use, production activation or gate change follows from this review.

## Reconciliation with inherited doctrine

No WHY conflict found with Constitution Articles II/III/VII and Profession Model
chapters 2/7/8: traceability, attention, human judgment and no invented certainty
are reinforced. Explicit qualified hypotheses do not weaken the prohibition on
implicit hypotheses presented as observations. Conditional human decisions already
proven in V1 remain intact; the review authorizes no automatic decision escalation.

Implementation gaps are not doctrinal repeal: current SourceFact/UnderstandingResult
does not fully represent comparability dimensions or conflicting reported/derived
lineage. Annual COMPARABLE means only ANNUAL_LABEL_ARITHMETIC_ONLY with financial
comparability NOT_ESTABLISHED. It must not be read as professionally comparable.
Citation existence/value validation is not full semantic reasoning validation.
The existing financial_doctrine.py v0 retains its bounded role and is not silently
replaced by this broader professional reasoning contract.
