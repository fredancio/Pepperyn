## Shared source conventions

- Currency EUR, figures literal euros, no thousands multiplier. Ratios below
  are percentages only where explicitly identified; pp means percentage points.
- Each case is a separate synthetic engagement. Line references such as
  FR01.S1 are local to that case, never an authorization to share between cases.
- Annual comparisons use 1 January–31 December 2025 and 2026, same entity,
  perimeter and conventions ONLY where the case explicitly states that.
- A fact copied from a source is not a verified accounting reconciliation.
- Monetary source values/differences have zero tolerance here. Ratios may be
  rounded to 0.01 percentage point; retain numerator/denominator provenance.

  represented as a confirmed decision, or a forecast presented as an observation.

  is not an independent professional reviewer. No identity/date/signature inferred.
