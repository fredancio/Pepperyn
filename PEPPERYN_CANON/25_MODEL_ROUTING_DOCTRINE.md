# MODEL ROUTING DOCTRINE

Status: ACTIVE — explicit Founder decision, recorded as DEC-033.
Scope: development-agent routing, not Pepperyn's external-provider admission.

## Default execution owner

**Default model: Sol, medium reasoning effort.**

Sol owns execution within an established architecture, doctrine, invariant set
and acceptance contract. Critical or security-sensitive work does not
automatically require Astra when its invariants and PASS/FAIL contract are
already explicit. Both models remain bound by the autonomy contract in 18,
the No Silent Regression contract in 11 and all applicable Founder gates.

## Escalation from Sol to Astra

Escalate when progress requires at least one of:

- establishing an unknown cause after bounded investigation;
- choosing between materially different architectures;
- creating or materially modifying doctrine, invariants or trust boundaries;
- resolving a systemic contradiction;
- changing the semantics or acceptance criteria of a governance gate;
- approving a materially irreversible architectural change;
- introducing a new structural primitive not already governed by the Canon;
- resolving a security/privacy boundary whose correct treatment is not already
  specified;
- or two unsuccessful bounded attempts on the same unresolved problem.

A governance control blocking execution must never be weakened solely to avoid
escalation. Record the bounded attempts and observed failure before handing off;
do not disguise the same unresolved problem as a new task to reset the count.

Model escalation is not Founder approval. Where the autonomy contract reserves
a decision to the Founder, Astra may investigate and prepare a recommendation,
but cannot approve or execute the reserved change on the Founder's behalf.

## Return from Astra to Sol

**Astra owns uncertainty resolution, not routine execution.**

Return as soon as all four conditions are satisfied:

**cause understood + solution selected + invariants explicit + PASS/FAIL criteria
defined.**

Astra hands the bounded implementation/proof work back to Sol rather than
continuing routine execution. The handoff must identify the evidence for the
cause, selected solution, protected invariants, acceptance/refusal conditions
and any outstanding Founder authorization. A handoff is not permission to cross
an outstanding gate. Do not claim a runtime model switch merely because the
documentation names the next execution owner.

## Governance is independent of model capability

**Model capability must never substitute for missing governance.**

Model choice must not modify Pepperyn's doctrine, evidence requirements,
security boundaries, fail-closed behavior or Founder approval gates. Existing
authority, restrictions and operating-loop duties apply equally to Sol and
Astra; historical Astra takeover records remain preserved as provenance.

This decision changes no substantive B1 architecture or acceptance criterion.
B1 remains OPEN and the genuine generic producer remains UNADMITTED.
Generic Producer Admission is neither External Provider Admission nor Real-data
Admission. External Provider and Real-data Admission remain CLOSED; Self-Selling
remains DEFERRED. This doctrine authorizes no provider call, real data, remote
mutation, deployment or replay of a historical rehearsal.
