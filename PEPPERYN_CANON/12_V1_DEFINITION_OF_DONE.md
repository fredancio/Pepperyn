explicit, revision-bound evidence. “The agent believes it works” is not PASS.

## Golden Private-Beta workflow

Frédéric and one authorized Fractional CFO can manage distinct client companies,
import admitted financial data, obtain professionally usable and traceable
analysis, retain client-specific context, return with a later period, understand
change, retrieve prior decisions/actions and identify where attention belongs,
without cross-client or cross-user contamination and without routine technical
manipulation.

## Stop condition

No Private Beta production declaration or real-data use is authorized until the
relevant gates above are individually evidenced as PASS and the Founder has
approved the release/admission actions reserved to him.
