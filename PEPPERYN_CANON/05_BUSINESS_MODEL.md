# Business model

## Current validated hypothesis

The current business hypothesis is capacity and quality leverage for Fractional
CFO work: manage more SMEs correctly, or the same portfolio materially better,
with improved continuity and lower cognitive load.

## What is not yet decided for V1

- pricing;
- subscription packaging;
- public go-to-market;
- sales funnel;
- autonomous commercial execution;
- final production legal/operator presentation.

Legacy Stripe, quota and upgrade capabilities exist in the repository. They are
not evidence that Private Beta must use them and must not drive V1 scope.

The future Self-Selling Control Center remains preserved but parked. It cannot
be interpreted as part of the Private Beta Definition of Done.
