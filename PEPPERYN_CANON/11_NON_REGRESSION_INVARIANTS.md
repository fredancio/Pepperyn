# No Silent Regression contract

## Rule

> Astra may improve or replace implementation, but must not silently reduce an
> existing Pepperyn capability, epistemic guarantee, privacy guarantee,
> security guarantee, professional requirement or Founder decision.

## Required change record for material work

Every material change must state:

- **BEFORE:** capability and guarantee that currently exist;
- **CHANGE:** what implementation is being modified;
- **AFTER:** equivalent or stronger capability and guarantee;
- **EVIDENCE:** tests, falsification, benchmark or live proof;
- **OPEN DEBT:** what the evidence does not prove.

## Critical invariants

- LLM output is never canonical truth automatically.
- facts != inference != hypothesis != recommendation != confirmed decision;
- `UNKNOWN` and `CONTRADICTION` remain first-class;
- provenance survives to terminal use;
- tenant, user, company, entity and engagement ownership fail closed;
- temporal continuity and durable decision memory are preserved;
- correspondence is stable only within its authorized continuity boundary;
- cross-client pseudonym correlation is denied by default;
- raw identity and correspondence mappings remain outside providers;
- provider disclosure is task-bound, minimal and receipted;
- provider response is quarantined as untrusted input;
- rehydration requires explicit local authority;
- `REIDENTIFIED` content is terminal-only and cannot re-enter egress;
- pseudonymized data is not called anonymous while re-identification exists;
- provider compliance is defense-in-depth, not a bypass of privacy controls;
- no real financial data before Real-data Admission PASS;
- no external provider before the entire Provider Gate PASS;
- no ActualOutcome/Learning without genuinely later evidence;
- no retroactive ExpectedImpact fabrication;
- no public signup, sales, pricing or Stripe requirement for Private Beta V1;
- Self-Selling remains parked until its explicit reopening criteria are met;
- active Pepperyn surfaces must not present Pepperyn as a Finflate product.

## Founder escalation

Explicit Founder approval is required to weaken/remove a critical invariant or
to change the North Star, professional doctrine, privacy/security guarantee,
tenant isolation, real-data admission, provider activation, production release
gate, material product/business model, legal/operator identity, or to perform a
destructive/irreversible production action.
