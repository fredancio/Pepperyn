# Product model

## Primary user and job

The initial primary user is a Fractional CFO managing several SMEs. Pepperyn
must reduce reconstruction effort and answer: where is this company now, what
changed, what deserves attention, what was decided, what remains unresolved and
what professional action should come next?

## Primary surfaces

- portfolio and intelligent client cards;
- governed financial ingestion and analysis;
- history and temporal comparison;
- evidence-linked recommendations and required validations;
- intention, explicit professional decision, follow-up and execution memory;
- governed client deliverables in XLSX, PDF and PPTX;
- administrative Control Center, separate from client experience.

## Product success test

Every V1 capability must contribute to safer capacity, better continuity, lower
cognitive load, higher analysis quality or traceable professional judgment.
Demo completeness alone is not product value.

## Private Beta boundaries

Two authorized professionals, no public signup, no commercial sale, no pricing
or Stripe dependency, and no real data until admission. The existing commercial
and billing code is inherited capability, not a Private Beta requirement.
